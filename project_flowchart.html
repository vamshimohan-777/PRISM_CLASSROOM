Deterministic Rubric Scoring & Pedagogical Safeguards

PRISM replaces black-box probabilistic models with a deterministic, mathematically verifiable rubric model:

Mastery = round(clamp(0.05, 0.95, 0.25 * M_prev + 0.75 * (Σ w_i · c_i / Σ w_i)), 2)
  • w_i = 1.0: Standard diagnostic and scaffolded practice items.
  • w_i = 1.4: Independent transfer verification questions (40% greater evidentiary weight).
  • c_i ∈ {0, 1}: Exact correctness indicator.
  • Bounded: Constrained strictly within [0.05, 0.95] to prevent over-confidence or irrecoverable zero-scores.

Pedagogical Safeguard Rules

  1. Single-Error Protection: An isolated error NEVER assigns a misconception label. Requires at least 2 matching tagged errors.
  2. Confidence Floor: Misconception confidence must be ≥ 50% relative to total errors before visual intervention is prescribed.
  3. Teacher Review Fallback: Contradictory evidence (e.g. 1 error in concept A, 1 in concept B) routes learner to "follow-up" (Teacher Conference Pod).
